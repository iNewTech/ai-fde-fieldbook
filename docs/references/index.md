# Video and reading library

Every numbered lesson contains its own relevant video and primary reading. This page is the detailed reference shelf; the [resource hub](/resources/) also includes the supplied roadmap PDF and reusable mentor skill. Links point to original teaching; videos, transcripts and paid exercises are not republished here. Some video-course hosts require an account or subscription. Older lectures explain durable concepts; verify current model names, APIs, prices and deployment settings in official documentation.

## Original papers

- [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762): attention and the transformer architecture.
- [Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401): retriever and generator formulation.
- [ReAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629): reasoning/action interaction.
- [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) and [QLoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314): parameter-efficient adaptation.
- [CLIP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020): aligned language and image representations.

Read a paper by stating its problem, mechanism, assumptions, experiment and limitation. Reproduce one small implication or calculation before trying to memorize the entire paper.

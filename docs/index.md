# FDE Fieldbook

<p class="lead">Learn to turn an enterprise problem into an AI system you can build, deploy, measure, and defend.</p>

<Roadmap />

## Your starting point

Begin with the [study plan](/plan/) and [readiness diagnostic](/guide/diagnostic). When you are ready to study in chat, say **"Start module 01 from FDE Fieldbook."** Preparation of this library does not start your lessons or mark any work complete.

The target is **20 weeks of study plus 2 weeks of revision**, at 15 hours per week. This is a planning estimate, not a promise. Beginners should spend extra time on foundations when the diagnostic shows a gap. Completing the plan builds a portfolio and evidence of competence; it does not guarantee a job or mastery of every tool.

## What you will learn

You will learn how an FDE turns a real enterprise problem into a useful, safe and measurable AI deployment:

- Discover workflows, users, constraints and business value before choosing technology.
- Build with Python, APIs, SQL, data pipelines, prompts, RAG, search, tools and agents.
- Design for identity, tenant isolation, evaluation, observability, latency, cost and recovery.
- Work with text, voice and video when the workflow genuinely needs multimodal interaction.
- Deploy, roll out, support and improve systems with customer stakeholders.
- Explain trade-offs and defend your architecture in an FDE interview or customer review.

## How to study here

The website is your structured study library. Read one lesson, watch the linked reference, open the Q&A only after attempting the questions, use the collapsed project helper when you are stuck, and complete the mini-project from its skeleton. Then come to an AI provider with the reusable [AI/FDE Mentor skill](/guide/mentor-skill), paste the skill into its instructions or upload it where supported, and ask it to teach the current module. The AI chat is where you submit your explanation, code, design and test evidence for assessment.

The learning loop is: **read → predict → implement → test → explain → submit → receive critique → retake failed checks → advance only after passing**. Multiple-choice practice is useful feedback, but the mentor's review of your reasoning and project is the real gate.

Start with [How to study](/guide/how-to-study), then use the [readiness diagnostic](/guide/diagnostic). New learners should not skip the diagnostic.

## The work you will do

A support team receives thousands of requests, its policies contradict each other, and its CRM is full of duplicates. A customer asks for an AI agent. Your job starts with deciding which problem is worth solving.

Across the fieldbook, you will scope that workflow, build a grounded assistant, add controlled tools, harden the deployment, and evaluate voice and video interfaces. Each project includes a customer-facing deliverable and an engineering artifact. Cases use fictional organizations and synthetic data.

## A lesson is a decision you can defend

Read the explanation. Work through the example. Predict what breaks. Implement the exercise from its interface. Then answer the questions without looking back. Every module has a relevant video reference, an independent task, and a mastery gate.

English is the default. Ask **"Explain this in Hindi-English"** whenever that helps. Technical terms and assessment standards stay consistent.

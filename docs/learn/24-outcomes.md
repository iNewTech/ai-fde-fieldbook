# 24. Outcomes and portfolio defense

Week 20 | Prerequisite: 23 | Output: P8 demo, runbook and readiness review

## The enterprise problem

The assistant was used 5,000 times. The customer asks whether it saved time, improved quality or simply added another screen. An interviewer asks what you personally built and which trade-off you would change.

## First principles

Activity, adoption and value are different. Activity counts interactions. Adoption measures sustained use in the intended workflow. Value compares relevant outcomes against a baseline while including review effort, errors and operating cost. Track eligible cases and completion rates, not just total chat messages.

A before/after comparison can be confounded by changing case mix, staffing or seasonality. Where feasible, use a staged or controlled comparison; otherwise label the limitation. Synthetic benchmarks establish behavior under their test conditions, not realized customer savings. Keep observed measurements distinct from estimates and target thresholds.

An executive demo starts with the workflow, baseline, observed outcome and limitation. Show one representative success and one controlled failure. A technical defense adds architecture, alternatives, evaluation methodology and recovery evidence. Be specific about your contribution and about what is still incomplete.

A usable handover gives the next operator deployment steps, dependencies, dashboards, escalation contacts, rollback, known limitations and a simple user workflow. Documentation is successful when someone else can follow it; rehearse that transfer rather than judging the document by length.

## Trace an example

Handling time drops from eight to five minutes, but new review work takes four minutes on half the cases. Gross savings are three minutes per case; expected review cost is two, leaving one minute before operating costs. If harder cases moved out of the sample, even that comparison may overstate improvement.

## Independent work

Prepare a five-minute executive demo and a fifteen-minute architecture defense. Publish sanitized portfolio artifacts, measured synthetic results and explicit limitations. Have a reviewer use the runbook to reproduce the demo and recover from a simulated failure. Complete a mixed question session from all 24 modules and record remaining gaps honestly.

## Required checks

1. Distinguish usage, adoption and workflow value.
2. Calculate net benefit and identify confounders.
3. Explain personal implementation decisions and rejected alternatives.
4. Demonstrate a reproducible handover and recovery drill.
5. Pass mixed conceptual and practical checks without relying on memorized answers.

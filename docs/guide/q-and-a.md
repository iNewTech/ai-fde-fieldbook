# Questions and answers

## Is this an IIT Delhi course?

No. This is an independent study repository informed by the supplied brochure's curriculum and public engineering resources. It provides no institutional credential, private lab access, instructor affiliation or job guarantee. The brochure itself is not redistributed.

## Can I finish faster than six months?

The target is about five months at 15 hours per week, including two buffer weeks. Your engineering experience supports this pace. Independent implementation and successful gates determine the actual duration. Speed comes from reusing a project and testing existing skills, not removing difficult topics.

## Do I need to watch every video?

No. Each lesson identifies a relevant video or video course and a focus. Use about 30 minutes in the weekday video slot and selected additional sections only when needed. Many course hosts require an account or paid features. Free primary readings remain linked. We link to original videos rather than copy transcripts or lessons.

## Will the mentor write the project for me?

No. You get interfaces, TODOs, tests and progressive hints. You implement and defend the result. The docs website is already implemented so your study time can focus on engineering.

## Does completing the question bank pass a module?

No. Multiple-choice questions provide immediate practice feedback. The five open-ended questions in each lesson and the independent practical task form the actual gate. The mentor must see your evidence.

## Why start with discovery when I already code?

An FDE must establish whose workflow improves, what is slowing it down and how success is measured. A correct service that addresses the wrong bottleneck can waste a deployment. Discovery is an engineering input, not a meeting ritual.

## Is RAG necessarily a vector database?

No. Retrieval can use SQL, keywords, vectors, APIs or a combination. A vector store is one implementation choice. Data authorization, freshness, relevance and grounding remain necessary whichever retriever you use.

## Are multimodal systems always better than text?

No. Voice is useful when speaking fits the workflow; video is useful when the evidence is visual or temporal. Each adds latency, consent, evaluation and failure-handling work. Choose the interface based on the user and task.

## Does an agent mean multiple models?

No. The key is a system that uses model decisions to choose actions within a controlled loop. Multiple specialists can be useful, but they can also share mistakes and increase cost. Compare them against a fixed workflow and a single-agent baseline.

## Can I use my employer's data?

The exercises use synthetic fixtures. Access to an employer system is not assumed or needed. For an actual customer deployment, use explicit authorization, approved data boundaries and sanitized evidence.

## How do we study in Hindi-English?

Ask for Hindi-English at any point. The mentor can switch the explanation while preserving English identifiers and technical definitions. Your answers may use either language; the same understanding checks apply.

## Will lessons unlock every week?

All content is published together for simplicity. Personal progress is tracked privately and gated in chat. A static site's hidden navigation is not secure access control, especially when the repository is public.

## What about the enterprise-solutions startup?

After the capstone, use deployment evidence to examine repeated customer problems, implementation cost, support burden and willingness to pay. The current plan prioritizes study and a defensible portfolio.

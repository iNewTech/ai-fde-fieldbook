# How to study here

This fieldbook is designed for learners who want practical AI and Forward Deployed Engineering skills, whether they are beginners, software engineers, analysts or technical customer-facing professionals.

## The two-part setup

Use the website for the **curriculum** and an AI provider for the **mentor**.

The website contains the sequence, explanations, videos, references, answered Q&A, mini-projects, project helpers, system-design checkpoints and public practice questions. The AI provider gives you a conversational teacher that can ask follow-up questions, review your work and enforce the mastery gate.

Start with [What you will learn](/), the [study plan](/plan/) and the [readiness diagnostic](/guide/diagnostic). Then study one module at a time.

## The study loop

1. Read the current lesson in English. Ask the mentor for Hinglish when a concept is difficult.
2. Before opening answers, write what you think the Q&A means and predict one failure mode.
3. Watch the linked video selectively. Use the primary reading when the topic needs deeper precision.
4. Read the mini-project requirements. Open **Project helper** only after making your first design attempt.
5. Implement from the provided skeleton. Do not copy a complete solution; leave TODOs for your own work.
6. Run tests, record evidence and write the trade-off you made.
7. Send the explanation, design, code, tests and uncertainty to your AI mentor.
8. Take the module assessment. You need 100% on the multiple-choice test plus a pass on every required conceptual and practical check.
9. Retry only the failed areas with a changed example. Do not advance until the mentor explicitly passes the module.
10. Integrate the finished topic work into the next milestone project.

## Daily rhythm

For a 15-hour week, use one hour on each weekday for reading, recall, design, implementation and testing. Use the weekend for the longer build, review, mentor conversation and project evidence. A slower pace is acceptable; skipping the gate is not.

## What to send the mentor

Use this structure:

```text
I am studying FDE Fieldbook module: [module name]
I attempted the Q&A and project without opening the answers.
Here is my explanation:
Here is my design or code:
Here are my tests and results:
Here is one failure mode:
Here is one trade-off:
Please review critically. Do not give me a complete implementation.
```

## Important boundary

The public website cannot securely hide future lessons. Its collapsed answers and browser-based score are learning aids. The AI mentor's review is authoritative because it checks whether you can explain, implement, test and defend the work independently.
